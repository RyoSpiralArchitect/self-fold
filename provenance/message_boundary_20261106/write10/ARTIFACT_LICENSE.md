# Conditions for actual-report and retention experiments

The fitted arrays and experimental trajectories retain the project's Gemma-derived
artifact conditions under the [Gemma Terms of Use](GEMMA_TERMS.html) and
[Prohibited Use Policy](GEMMA_PROHIBITED_USE_POLICY.html).

reports are retained as experimental evidence; no base checkpoint is distributed.
