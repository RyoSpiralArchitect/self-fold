## Supported operation and independent scoring

A first-person policy names a regular action, an alternate, the condition that triggers the alternate, and a condition that cancels it or states that no cancellation applies. Resolving the existing cancellation makes its consequence explicit. The requested operation is identical across policies. No new instruction supplies a trigger, desired action, veto mask or later world to G. Source, identity, updated and unrelated states are judged against the *same* complete original policy. A separate Boolean reference enumerates all four worlds from the authored facts before any model output. When the trigger and cancellation overlap, a base table omitting the cancellation needs a change. No cancellation and logically disjoint cancellation are two kinds of justified stability.

Each topic has two familiar actions. Cross four single-literal triggers (left/right, on/off), five cancellations (the same four literals or absent), two action polarities, two action-clause orders and two fresh styles across eight known menus. The primary structured panel thus has 1,280 source states and 10,240 decisions with both label orders. Four other styles supply training. Keep all combinations and their deterministic ledger. Prefix each field with the same neutral sentence as an additional position control. Report group-macro scores and paired 95% percentile intervals across the eight topic clusters (10,000 draws, seed 4719). The fresh sentence styles change surface wording, not the underlying rule family.
