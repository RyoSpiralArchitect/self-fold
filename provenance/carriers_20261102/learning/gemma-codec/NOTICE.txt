The encoder_step_*.npz files in this directory are SELF-FOLD research encoder
artifacts created for use with Gemma-2-2B-IT. The low-rank encoder parameters
were newly initialized or trained by SELF-FOLD. They are not unmodified Google
pretrained checkpoint files. Training details and modifications are recorded
in each artifact's metadata_utf8 field and the adjacent validation receipts.
