# Can a source-specific training signal make a stored policy usable?

Prospective development experiment. The parent is `c280142`. The earlier 64-update fit and its opposed-donor audit are closed. This is a fresh two-arm comparison. Freeze this document and the executable source in Git before running on pretrained weights. The question came from a failure to distinguish the correct donor from its opposite despite improved reconstruction. [Earlier evidence](../carriers_20261102/RESULTS.md)

## Material and intervention

Use the public finite-policy corpus, 48 train groups (192 descriptions) and 24 exposed dev groups (96 descriptions). A pair holds site, family, wording and surface prefix fixed and reverses the tray assignment. The two wordings vary the introduction and share the policy clause. The dev families are scope (the training conditional construction with a different flag) and priority (an override rule). The 12 sites per family are template replications. The outcome concerns these constructions and two tray words. The separate 24-group qualification commitment remains sealed.

Use the pinned Gemma-2-2B-IT, eager BF16, frozen weights, pre-cut 13, K=8 workspace rows from a 256-token description-only forward. Capture and hash every source before constructing any recipient task. Cache each donor once and reuse it in both arms and both option orders. D is the frozen host. Read only the two public splits. Operator targets, questions and qualification content supply no training labels.

* **Copy** minimizes mean source-token NLL including native end-of-turn 107.
* **Copy + opposition** adds binary cross entropy of the correct versus opposed token at the first tokenizer divergence. Both poles have exactly the same preceding tokens, the same total target length and the same unordered token pair. Verify that these alternatives are the single tokens for copper and silver in this context. The two terms use the very same causal forward. Both arms compute both terms, with coefficients 0 and 1. The second term gives a rare, donor-dependent decision the weight of one sequence loss.
* Each arm independently initializes the same rank-16 residual bridge (73,728 parameters). Use AdamW, learning rate 0.0003, zero weight decay, betas (0.9, 0.999), epsilon 1e-8, global norm clip 1. Seed 47 shuffles all 96 train pairs without replacement in successive cycles. Four pairs give eight descriptions per update, both donors occur in every pair. Exactly 64 steps per arm. Check identical parameter start, ordered row IDs, training forwards and token totals. Step 64 is the only endpoint. No dev selection. Record each loss, gradient norm, immutable source/data bindings and both final artifacts.

## Three readouts of the same source

1. Fit a nearest-centroid readout on flattened **raw** train slots to predict the first-mentioned tray. Use Euclidean distance to the two class means, prior weights equal, no tuning. A fixed sham reverses labels in exactly six of the twelve sites within each train family (seed 237), both poles and phrasings together. Report individual held-out decisions and within-pair ordering. Ordering can succeed even when an absolute decision boundary transfers poorly.
2. At each final bridge, record whole-source NLL and the signed preference for the correct first differing token on all 96 dev descriptions. Independently replay the decision in the inference host with the target suffix from the divergence onward replaced by placeholders at the same length. Require margin agreement within 1e-5 for every case. A strict lexical switch needs correct opposite signs for both donors sharing a prefix. Ties count against success. Report both families, 48 pairs total.
3. Ask the frozen native recipient about every truth-ledger world in both displayed label orders. Score A/B as in the first fit. Read the adapted state, literal source, empty input, zero slots, a single seeded random state at train mean row norm (seed 9292), and one fixed unrelated train donor (conditional-00-plus-p0). Apply each bridge to latent controls. Preserve all 576 logical questions per route and arm, including repeated controls. Deterministic identical question/state calls may reuse their exact scores. Cache keys include the full question and state identity. Verify native parity and neutral replay for every distinct slot-question geometry. Report group-macro correctness, strict correct opposed switches, joint correctness across the two wrappers and switches across worlds whose gold actions differ. Report all family strata and ties.

A strict opposed switch holds the world, wrapper and order fixed and requires **both** donors correct. A same-meaning pair holds the policy, world and order fixed and requires **both** wrappers correct. A world switch holds the policy, wrapper and display order fixed and needs both answers correct on a pair of worlds with differing gold. Compute rates within each group before equal weighting of the 24 groups. Report paired arm differences with the two families separately. For the primary accuracy difference also give a descriptive percentile interval over 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples of sites within family (seed 47). It captures site variation conditional on the two templates.

## Decisions fixed before fitting

| Observation | Condition to finish or advance |
| --- | --- |
| Invalid engineering | Stop at the first parity, hash, pairing, isolation, numerical, input-budget or schedule failure. Keep partial evidence and identify the affected comparison. |
| Functional access | An arm needs ≥80% macro accuracy, ≥10 points over the strongest of its four fixed nulls, ≥80% strict opposed switches and ≥90% joint wrapper correctness. Show world switches as an additional diagnostic. Advance a passing arm to D/E roundtrip and positional controls. Prefer copy if both pass. Attribute a practical gain to the opposed loss only if its accuracy exceeds copy by ≥10 points in this paired run. |
| Lexical access only | ≥80% strict prefix-pair switches, but the functional screen fails. Close this training objective at step 64. Next isolate rule application with a train-only truth-ledger recipient and new paraphrases that move or reverse the action mentions. |
| Weak native lexical access | If neither arm meets either screen, check the train-only raw-slot probe. ≥80% absolute dev accuracy and ≥10 points above its sham motivates testing a different readout on the same carrier. Otherwise test capture/geometry with a separate, finite design. A negative probe bounds this readout only. |
| After a usable carrier | Freeze the carrier, measure D and E(D) separately, then test one G step against identity on change-required and justified no-change cases. Blind reviewers judge support and a useful new distinction. Require the six reviewed checks on sealed qualification before a short paired L/B pilot. |

Limit this cycle to two fits, one seed and one evaluation of the fixed endpoints. No free-generation endpoint is scored here. The older EOS/censoring policy applies to future free readouts. A correct first tray can be recovered by lexical order, and two wrappers share a clause. Passing this screen would still leave independent semantic support, one-step updating, self/other controls and later reasoning transfer to establish.
