# Topic words in questions and verdict position in sources

Section 6.14 compared questions that differed in several ways and sources with different grammar and evaluative vocabulary. This study varies one displayed topic word or the order of two fields. It freezes the previously calibrated renderer and the fitted source reader at their published hashes. All eight topics and four familiar action pairs per topic stay in scope. Use one pinned Gemma-2-2B-IT, zero optimizer steps and two independent endpoints. No new-topic, learned-G or qualification data enter this study.

## Correct lexical teacher and matched questions

Four newly authored generic question stems each have four versions. Bare presents the stem alone. Topic adds a single neutral topic word on the line immediately above the stem. Placebo uses General on that line. Rotated uses the next topic word in the registered eight-topic order, wrapping at the end. The same four stems and option phrases are used throughout. The topic word does not specify a pole. A tokenizer-only preflight checked these fixtures before scoring. For each topic, stem and option order, the three named prompts must differ only at one native token. Removing that token and the following newline must recover the complete bare prompt. Record the token position and IDs, lengths and prompt hashes.

Use the true canonical lexical policy through the frozen Section 6.14 renderer and the Section 6.11 lexical transformation. The common mean teacher follows the same path. Present both opposing teachers and both option orders. Each condition has 128 unique policy decisions on eight topics. Both label orders for one subject and stem are scored together, as in Section 6.14. Record full-vocabulary greedy accuracy, forced A/B, coverage, ties, strict topics and opposed-margin flips. Count a strict topic only when all sixteen judgments are right. Each opposed pair contributes once to the flip count.

The primary effect compares the correct topic heading with bare on the same 128 trials. To support a topic-specific benefit require a net gain of at least 10 percentage points over bare, at least five points over each of placebo and rotated, positive topic-minus-bare counts in at least three of four stems and five of eight topics. Report all paired gains and losses even when this conjunction fails. Independently apply the previous reliability criteria to the four correctly cued families. Require at least 95% overall and in each family, seven strict topics, 90% of 64 opposed margins reversed, and no more than 50% correct under the common null. Retain the earlier fitting result only after the published renderer and prior fresh scores replay. Report signed logit-margin shifts and deletion of one topic at a time as descriptive checks. No template, topic or question can be removed.

## Question-free source and matched field swaps

Use two explicit preference records for each source. Each record contains a verdict field and an action field. In the before version, the verdict precedes the action. In the after version, swap the two entire fields in both records. Brackets, record prefix, word case, punctuation, action phrases and exact verdict words are fixed. Use the cue pairs “I accept this” / “I reject this” and “my first choice” / “my last choice”. Cross two cue pairs, four familiar expression pairs, eight topics, both preferred poles and both clause orders. This yields 256 paired placements, 512 new E states. Every swap must preserve the complete token multiset and all eight workspace positions on the actual encoder prompt. Record the position, length and source identity checks.

The frozen Section 6.14 reader takes only one E tensor. Record inferred topic, first action, first-clause role, complete policy from the product of the signs, direct polarity, first-mention shortcut and the oracle-topic factor. Count both component signs correct only when the inferred topic and both signs are correct. A double error can yield a correct policy. Report that cell separately. Retain strict units across both orders/poles and correctly directed opposed pairs under both fixed role order and fixed action mention order. As a readability control, give the literal source to the frozen base using the first new bare question in both label orders. This yields 512 literal choices per placement and 1,024 overall.

For the primary source contrast, compare before and after on the same 256 sources. Register a directional effect in either direction only when both complete policy and both-components-correct differ by at least ten percentage points in that direction, both differences are nonnegative within each cue vocabulary and each clause order in that orientation, and at least five of eight topics favor that direction on joint components. To describe the effect as specific to the slot reader on these records, additionally require at least 90% literal accuracy in each placement and no more than five points of difference between the literal conditions. Report the paired effect and this readability check separately. A missed contrast leaves direction unresolved by this rule. Independently report the previous source screen by vocabulary and placement at 90% complete policy, 75% strict and 90% correct opposed pairs under each exchange, with the original frozen fitting result. These two-field records bound the claim to this format.

## Execution and closure

Verify the sixteen teacher hashes, base identity and native parity. Before scoring any fresh model endpoint, replay all 128 previous fresh calibrated native choices including their stored A/B log probabilities in the same two-label batch layout, and all 256 previous fresh E hashes, inferred topics and factor/direct/mention predictions. Perform the tokenizer and slot-position checks on every matched pair. Stop and preserve a technical failure if a binding, replay or alignment fails. Zero updates, model-forward limit 4,000, four million processed tokens and 1,200 seconds from host initialization. Preserve paired numbers, raw inputs or their deterministic fixture keys, raw native scores and all controls. Leave-one-topic-out ranges describe this bank and are not confidence intervals. Report boundary failures without retuning. These component experiments do not execute source-to-native composition, G or the EOS language intervention.
