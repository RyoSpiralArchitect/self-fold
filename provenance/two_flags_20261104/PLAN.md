# From two flags to an action

Can explicit intermediate supervision help the same small reader apply a policy under changed wording? A prior literal recipient reached 3,751 of 3,840 fitting choices. Eighty-five of its 89 errors were gold A predicted as B. A second, separable question is whether an externally specified one-bit intermediate can carry a subsequent choice and support controlled reversal. This prospective development comparison commits both questions and their success rules before any scores from the new panel.

## Materials and information boundaries

Both arms start from the exact margin-continuation endpoint `91b7e96a22c43d6c9e9b626b8d4c3131a2d9a563bc7a5191f9a9517062727845` on the pinned Gemma-2-2B-IT host. Use all eight known action topics, three familiar pairs of indicator names, two familiar wording families, both policies, both genuine source and table orders and one canonical rule order. Each of the 480 fitting descriptions has four worlds and both displayed answer orders. The fitting action questions are byte-identical to the previous study.

The new assessment crosses familiar or new flag names, source grammar and question wording in a 2 × 2 × 2 design. Each cell uses two name pairs and one source family. The familiar level uses the first two fitting pairs, the first fitting family and the fitting question as applicable. Each cell has 160 descriptions, 1,280 choices and 16 strict units per stage. The familiar cell reuses fitting material. The jointly new cell is primary. Independent names, source sentences, question predicates and probe wording are fixed in `data/two_flags_20261104.json`. Formal meanings and action vocabulary stay fixed. Counts are repeated observations within eight topics. No old fresh score selects a checkpoint. Both new endpoints must be saved and replayed before any evaluation panel is scored.

## Matched training

`direct`, **Practise the final choice**, sees only ordinary literal action questions. `parts`, **Learn the parts of the choice**, allocates one quarter of its answers to each of four tasks. These are a reported flag value, the conjunction of the two flags, the action conditional on a supplied branch bit, and the ordinary action given the raw world. The flag position alternates. For flag and conjunction questions the reader sees the world facts and a neutral source with no policy or actions. For branch questions it receives the policy and a verbal report of one Boolean value with no current individual readings. Ordinary action questions contain the policy and the two raw world readings.

Give each arm 384 additional AdamW updates with new optimizer state, learning rate 0.0001, no weight decay and clip norm one. The only trainable weights are the parent's rank-16 query/value task adapters on the last four layers. Each arm receives exactly the same seeded schedule (411) of two topic/stage/name/style/order units per step, each with both poles. Cycle source, cases and rule equally. Every one of the four sources supplies four worlds and both label mappings, giving 32 decisions in eight microbatches of four. Assign component tasks by a fixed rotating schedule and keep complete label pairs together. Both arms have 12,288 target labels, 3,072 training forwards and identical padded context shapes at every microbatch. For each microbatch use the larger of the two arms' natural widths and right-pad both with masked tokens. Record actual unmasked token counts separately.

The shared objective keeps full-vocabulary cross entropy (world 11 weight three, others one, divisor 48) and the order-specific gold-versus-foil hinge at margin two (divisor 32). Add 0.1 times the mean smooth-L1 penalty on the half-sum of the two A-minus-B margins in a label-swapped pair (16 pairs per update). This gives both arms the same encouragement to map answer order correctly. The comparison estimates how allocating supervision among these tasks affects the endpoint at matched update, target, padded-token and forward budgets. Final-action exposure and unmasked language tokens can differ.

## Endpoints and bounded decisions

1. **Primary training comparison.** Score the ordinary one-forward, full-vocabulary action on the jointly new cell. The component-trained arm needs a paired gain of at least five percentage points (64 of 1,280), a positive net gain in at least five of eight topics, and at least as many complete strict units across the three stages. Record decisions recovered and lost. A strict unit fixes stage, topic, wording and name pair and must get both policies, every world, every genuine source order and both displayed label orders correct. Report all eight cell counts, all stages, A/B coverage, ties and the separate historical eligibility rules (fit at least 95% native and 90% strict, jointly new at least 90% native and 75% strict at each stage).
2. **Secondary constructive endpoint.** On the same jointly new material run a two-call literal interface. A world-only native answer to the conjunction question is decoded using its displayed label mapping into one bit. The next call receives only that bit's fixed verbal rendering and the literal policy. It receives no current pair of readings. Cross both first-call and second-call label orders independently. Compare the predicted bit, its complement, a supplied true bit and an opposed policy at fixed predicted bit. Score actual recorded native token outputs, memoizing identical first calls (two flag pairs × four worlds × two orders) and second calls (160 policies × two bits × two orders). The truth ledger enters scoring and the specified oracle condition. A non-A/B first answer makes all its predicted and exchanged paths fail. No forced choice or order averaging replaces it. Complete strict units now also cross the first-call label order. Report the true-bit oracle separately. The predeclared constructive claim concerns `parts`; report the same controls for `direct`. For a pass, each fresh component probe (first flag, second flag and conjunction) needs at least 90% native in each label order. At each stage require 90% final native, 75% strict, 90% jointly correct original plus bit-flipped judgments, and 90% jointly correct original plus opposed-policy judgments. The three stage denominators are 1,024, 1,024 and 512 composed paths; each is dependent on reused native calls. A clean world-probe result with failed action controls localizes the limitation after extraction of the world bit.

The two endpoints have separate interpretations. One measures ordinary action transfer at equal training budget. The other tests a deliberately exposed Boolean interface on a finite language task. Both endpoints close at the fixed cap irrespective of their signs. Record single-seed eight-topic scope. Keep literal, learned slot and recurrent evidence distinct. An independently confirmed source-state reader and stage-specific operator controls are still required before testing autonomous updates G1/G2. Future tests of model/data/depth scaling and unrelated abilities require held-out tasks and matched compute. This study trains no G, reads no qualification material and measures no broad capability gain. Hard run limits are 30,000 forwards, 20 million padded tokens and 2,400 seconds. Missing/nonfinite gradients or non-identical endpoint replay are technical failures. Retain the first terminal receipt.
