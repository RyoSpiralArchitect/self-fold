# Paired small-set recipient trainability control

The raw, standardized, binary-objective and jointly learned source/recipient development candidates closed below their recipient screen. The numerical audit found many rounded ties, while its FP32 diagnostics did not recover reliable development decisions. Before choosing another carrier architecture, this control asks whether the task output adapter can fit a small, fully specified set with either full literal evidence or the frozen raw carrier.

Select public training groups `both_if-01`, `either_if-01`, `unless-01`, `exclusive-01`, stage 2, self frame, both policy poles and both source-order tags, all four worlds, question wording zero and both label orders. These are 128 tasks. Select the first two development groups in committed order with the same cross, giving 64 diagnostic tasks. Stage-2 normalization can make the two order-tagged descriptions identical. The compound count therefore denotes the registered policy/order tags, not independent clause-order generalization.

Capture the 24 logical source rows before constructing any question. Exact source duplicates may share a capture. Require every source and raw-state digest to match the completed original recipient fit. The old qualification data and any future two-stage qualification material remain outside this loader.

Train two fresh task-only adapters on the same frozen Gemma base, last four layers q/v, rank 16, scale 1, initialization seed 47. One receives the literal stage-2 source in the normal native task prompt. The other receives its cached eight raw workspace slots. Both receive the later task question. Use the same repeated four-group shuffle cycles (seed 47), 64 updates, an effective batch of 32 and four microbatches of eight, AdamW learning rate 0.0003, zero weight decay, default betas/epsilon and global norm clip 1. The objective is binary cross entropy on the original native post-softcap A/B logits. Record zero gradient norms as observed optimization outcomes. A nonfinite gradient, disconnected parameter graph, altered backbone, identity mismatch or incomplete panel is invalid. There is no fallback candidate.

The task sets, order, initialization and update budget match across conditions. Literal prompts contain more input tokens, so actual forward/token costs are reported separately. Source text and task questions are available to the literal positive control by definition. They are never concatenated during raw carrier capture.

Measure initial and final native full-vocabulary greedy A/B correctness, forced A/B correctness, label coverage and exact ties on every training and diagnostic-development task. Also count joint native correctness over both poles and both source-order tags at fixed group, world and label mapping. Record all component decisions. A condition passes this engineering trainability check only if final training group-macro native accuracy is at least 95% and joint policy/order correctness at least 90%. Development is descriptive. No result here is a semantic qualification, a learned G composition or evidence about global model capacity.

Freeze both final artifacts without selecting between them. Verify serialization parity on the same first training microbatch, retain all 128 update records and 768 initial/final decision records, hash the backbone before and after, and stop after this paired control. G and independent qualification remain pending.
