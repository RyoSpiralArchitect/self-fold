## The frozen recipient comparison

We froze raw Gemma-2-2B-IT workspace E, eight slots at pre-cut13, from a description-only forward of256 tokens. The source and both teacher stages supply1,152 train variants and288 dev variants each, across96 train and24 dev groups. Construction grammars, action/flag words and sites are held out, while Boolean truth functions and target rendering grammars overlap. Source questions are constructed only after all4,320 logical donor records have been captured.

A task-scoped rank16 q/v adapter in the final four decoder layers minimizes full-vocabulary native A/B cross entropy for256 AdamW updates. Each effective batch has32 questions, balanced over both policies, both source orders, four worlds and both label mappings. Only the receiver is active in task forwards. A second candidate uses the same initialization, schedule and budget with fixed train-only coordinate standardization at task injection. Its execution was conditional on the raw candidate's fixed final endpoint missing the screen.

The raw stage2 candidate met52/1,152 units (4.51%), and standardized met17/1,152 (1.48%). Stage2 rendering is canonical and its two order tags are byte-identical. This compound counts two real opposed policies with duplicated order inputs. Clause-order robustness must be examined in source and case-stage panels, and eventually on the actual learned G chain.

Both candidates missed the registered90% native accuracy,10-point null advantage and75% joint screen. Their strongest fixed null scored50%. All native argmax outputs were A or B, but many A/B likelihoods tied exactly. In stage2 there were3,872 raw and3,238 standardized ties out of4,608, which counted against forced-choice success.
