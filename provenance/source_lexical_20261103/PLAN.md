## One ridge fit and one 64-update residual fit

Flatten each eight-row source. Compute featurewise means and standard deviations on the 32 training states only. The scale floor is 0.01 times the mean training standard deviation, with a minimum of 1e-8. Fit a centered linear map to their paired canonical lexical rows by dual ridge regression, using K = X X-transpose / feature_count and lambda = 0.001. Solve in float64 and retain FP32 buffers. The lexical output mean also uses training teachers only. This fixed ridge endpoint is the first candidate and reference.

Add a rank-16 residual map, (X A) B, with seed-47 random A and zero B. Train this residual alone for 64 updates at learning rate 0.0003 with AdamW, zero weight decay and clip 1. The frozen ridge map and lexical transform remain differentiable with respect to their input. Each update draws four topic/variant units from a shuffled seed-47 schedule, takes both poles in each unit, and uses both option assignments of question wording zero, for eight source examples and 16 task decisions. Microbatch size is four. The loss is conditional native A/B cross entropy plus 0.1 times mean squared lexical-teacher error divided by the fixed training teacher variance. Add 0.01 times the mean squared positive part of teacher batch standard deviation minus predicted batch standard deviation, divided by that same variance. Standard deviations use population variance plus 1e-8 before square root. This is a teacher-relative variance floor, with no reward for exceeding teacher variance. Average over source examples and answer mappings. Preserve gradients through the source mapper, fixed lexical transform and fixed native recipient. The inference-only State container stays detached, and is not used on the training gradient path.

Evaluate the ridge and the fixed 64-update endpoint. Save both, reload and check exact projected rows and checked native logits. There is no validation checkpoint choice or second residual candidate. Record actual forwards, tokens, losses and gradient norms. The arm has a 1,800-second wall cap and the host's 20,000-forward / 20-million-token caps. Retain any technical failure without retrying a scientific fit. Learning G and changing E, the lexical interface or base parameters are outside this fit.

## Measurements and decision rules

At each endpoint evaluate matching E states, the opposed pole at the same topic/phrasing, the training-mean E state, all-zero E rows, the same donor's reversed rows and fixed random directions with the source's per-row norms. Use the same tensors unchanged across both question wordings and answer assignments. Retain full-vocabulary greedy output, forced A/B preferences and ties. Also evaluate raw E, original literal descriptions and independently captured canonical lexical teachers through the same frozen recipient. Empty or non-A/B native answers are incorrect.

A strict pair for a topic/style requires both poles correct under both source-clause orders and every evaluated question wording and option assignment. Report per-source and cross-source-order results, the opposed-input margin sign-flip rate, and the null's signed option preference. The null may retain the frozen model's option bias, which is measured independently of donor discrimination. Report seen training source/seen question separately from the held-out question wording, the held-out source style within training topics, the two previous dev topics and the two new topics. Every source and every question keeps its denominator, including ties and failures.

The fixed endpoint's training screen requires at least 95% native accuracy and 90% strict pairs on the actual training questions. Within-topic withheld sources and new-topic sources each require at least 80% native accuracy, 75% strict pairs, a ten-percentage-point native advantage over the strongest mean/zero/norm null, and a thirty-percentage-point advantage over opposed E inputs under the original gold answers. Apply the same screen descriptively to the ridge reference. Admission of this interface requires all three screens. It supplies an engineering development result, preceding independent semantic qualification and two-stage recurrence.

End after the one ridge solve and 64 residual updates regardless of outcome. A failed literal or lexical ceiling localizes a recipient/task limitation. Good teacher readout with failed training-map readout localizes the source-to-lexical connection. A training pass with failed new-topic transfer localizes the generalization boundary. A full development pass permits freezing the E-to-recipient route for a separately sealed G experiment. The thresholds and current development sources remain fixed in every case.
