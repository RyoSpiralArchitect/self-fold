# Gemma-derived source-map artifacts

The two files ridge.npz and final.npz include statistics and maps derived from Gemma activations. They are distributed under the [Gemma Terms of Use](GEMMA_TERMS.html), including its use restrictions and the incorporated [Prohibited Use Policy](GEMMA_PROHIBITED_USE_POLICY.html). Use, reproduction, modification and distribution must comply with those conditions. The [modification notice](NOTICE.txt) and [copied terms provenance](terms-provenance.json) accompany them. Original SELF-FOLD software and synthetic material use [Apache-2.0](../../../LICENSE).

The source-map kind is centered_E_to_lexical_ridge_residual. Its metadata binds the original E capture, host, training material and fixed lexical interface. It accepts eight E rows and outputs eight rows before that lexical transformation. Use the source_mapping module and the sealed experiment script for its declared geometry.
