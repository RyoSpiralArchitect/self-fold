# Can a known policy be read reliably before and after source encoding?

This prospective development study follows the failed Section 6.13 screen. It has two independently judged endpoints on all eight known topics. Test the native recipient first, with the correct lexical policy supplied. Then read the topic and the chosen action directly from a question-free source on new sentence frames using familiar actions. Keep each result even if the other misses. The study cannot reopen the earlier joint wording screen or qualify G.

## Native recipient with the teacher supplied

Each of the 16 canonical lexical policies is captured without a question and must match its archived hash. Retain the Section 6.11 lexical interface and Gemma-2-2B-IT frozen. Start a single shared rank-four row renderer from the Section 6.13 four-style endpoint. Fit only its 18,432 parameters on correct lexical teachers and all four previously exposed question families, with both A/B assignments and both policies for every topic. Use full-vocabulary next-token cross entropy. At seed 47 separately shuffle the 32 topic/wording blocks each epoch, taking four blocks per update and both poles and label orders per block. Eight complete epochs give 64 AdamW updates at 0.0003, clip 1, no weight decay. No source E, fresh questions or answer-label side inputs enter the renderer. Always keep the last update. Check the serialized renderer on a fitting batch before any fresh scores.

Evaluate four sealed new wording families (two generic, two topic-specific) and both option orders. The 128 unique teacher decisions are 16 policies × four questions × two label orders, clustered in eight topics. Present every question to opposing teachers at identical displayed options. Record full-vocabulary greedy answers, forced A/B, coverage and ties. Include the archived canonical and archived rendered teachers, the new renderer and its common mean-teacher null. A strict topic gets every pole, question and label assignment right. Report each frame and all topics.

The single calibrated candidate passes if fitting and fresh native accuracy each reach 95%, every fresh family reaches 95% (31/32 or better), at least seven of eight fresh topics are strict, at least 90% of 64 unique fresh opposed-teacher margins change sign, and the fresh common null is at or below 50% native. No post-score selection of teachers, questions or topics. Archive raw keys, margins and weights.

## Source-only test of role binding

Cross all four previously fitted grammatical frames with each of four familiar matched action-expression pairs per topic, both chosen poles and both clause orders. This yields 512 fitting sources. The two newly sealed frames use exactly those same action phrases in both slots, producing 256 fresh sources. The question, topic, index of the pair, role, order and opposite source are absent at inference. Neither new words for the actions nor new topics enter this claim. The second frame places the evaluative words after the action phrase. Confirm that the two poles have identical word multisets at fixed frame and pair. Confirm that clause reversals change only order.

On fitting states alone, globally standardize each of the 18,432 flattened E features with the prior 1% scale floor. Fit centered dual ridge with penalty 0.01 times mean diagonal of the centered Gram, floor 1e-8, per solve. Store only feature directions, biases and fitting mean/scale. One global fit predicts the eight topic scores and whether the first clause accepts its action. Within each fitting topic, one two-output fit predicts whether the first action is plus and, for comparison, whether the accepted action is plus. The selected topic is inferred from E. The primary policy direction is the sign of first-action score times first-role score. When the preferred clause comes second the role sign reverses. The proposed direct affine polarity score in the predicted topic is the registered comparator. Report both, their two component accuracies and an oracle-topic diagnostic. Exact ties abstain. Compare the frozen Section 6.13 sixteen-policy mixture and a first-mention-only shortcut. No gradient tuning or selection on the fresh frames. Check exact score replay after serializing on fitting states.

For the primary source reader require 95% complete-policy accuracy and 90% strict units on fitting. On each fresh frame require at least 90% complete-policy accuracy, 75% strict units and 90% correctly directed opposed pairs under each of two exchanges. One exchange preserves the first clause's role, changing the first action. The other preserves the order of the two action phrases, changing which role each receives. Count each pair once. A strict topic/frame/action-pair unit requires the entire policy to be correct on both poles in both orders. Report each condition with its own denominator. The two factor outcomes, direct contrast and archived flat reader remain diagnostics even if they outperform the primary method.

## Conditional source-to-native evaluation and stop

Always record both independent screens. If both pass, hard-route each fresh E through its predicted one of the 16 lexical teachers and the frozen calibrated renderer on the four fresh questions, both label orders. Record matched and both opposed donor exchanges, mean-E and zero-E controls, and the correct-teacher oracle. An abstention uses the common mean teacher. Identical rendered inputs and prompts can reuse native scores provided each decision links to the actual scored input and prompt. Each fresh style must reach 90% native accuracy, 75% strict units, ten points over the stronger null and thirty over both opposed routes, scored against the original gold. If either component fails, close this composition without scoring it. A pass is a finite-policy development result. Stage-specific teacher reconstruction, operation omissions, continuous G states, intermediate exchange, paired EOS L/B and separate qualification still require their own seals.

One pinned model and seed. Record first failures. Ceiling 5,000 forwards, three million processed tokens and 1,200 seconds from host initialization for the model work. Use separate source hashes and endpoint receipts. Recompute the historical joint error table only from released 6.13 rows. Projection fractions from Section 6.12 remain specific to those states and the old span.
