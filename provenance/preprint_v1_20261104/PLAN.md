# Six histories, and a separate test of the raw reader

This development experiment starts from the frozen public manuscript at 231c3e5. The two studies have independent stopping rules. Fix this plan before collecting new model outputs. Retain all seeds, all arms and the first completed outcome. No qualification or confirmation material is opened.

## How many histories fit through the message?

Use the sixteen familiar action phrases in their published order. Triplet t comprises actions 2t, 2((t+1) mod 8)+1 and 2((t+3) mod 8). Fit on even t and test on odd t. The frozen Section 6 clause value head converts three independently encoded clauses to three soft action distributions. The three physical rows of the current table are exactly the same in all six histories of each triplet. Only a permutation of the three roles differs. The writer sees that supplied assignment at the preceding boundary. Only a discrete message and the identical table cross the boundary. The next instruction asks for the action previously assigned to one named role. The three instructions jointly distinguish all six histories. The next reader picks one row. Test every target against both remaining actions in both A/B orders with the frozen Section 6 native Gemma reader. All unordered pairs are queried for every possible emitted row before joining to gold. No new action phrases, natural-language role induction or novel role assignments are claimed.

Use one familiar clause syntax and one name pair for the fitting menus. The four test menus each use two fresh name pairs. All six assignments occur on both splits. Compare a supplied three-bit permutation code, erasure, and learned two- and three-bit channels. A complete history needs all three role addresses correct, or, for the separate native endpoint, all twelve decisions correct. For each fixed physical table the deterministic two-bit upper bound is four complete histories out of six. A single pairwise query common to two different target actions has opposite answers. Three bits suffice to distinguish six assignments but need not be learned.

Fit a categorical writer over 4 or 8 symbols from a one-hot encoding of the six supplied permutations and a categorical row reader per symbol and role. Maximize expected external native-choice reward on the fit menus, enumerating the categorical alternatives rather than sampling them. Seeds 17, 41 and 73, Gaussian initialization SD 0.2, Adam at 0.05, 512 full-distribution steps per endpoint. Freeze hard argmax writers and readers before compiling any test clauses or scoring test prompts. As a finite optimization reference, enumerate set partitions of the six histories with at most the available number of symbols and select the best row for each block and role by its fit reward. Break tied reward sums by complete fit histories, then by stable enumeration. The exhaustive reference and gradient learner are separate arms. Measure distinct written codes, address recovery, individual native wins and complete native histories, and substitute all-zero messages. Test menus are novel combinations of familiar actions. Native qualification for the supplied code requires at least 90% whole histories and 95% decisions. For a learned three-bit success require all six address histories in every seed and at least 90% whole native histories in every seed. The two-bit ceiling and optimization gaps are independently reportable outcomes.

## Can a reader use all three independently encoded stages?

Use the previous four-grammar fitting bank and its fixed eight-vector capture at Gemma cut 13, padded length 256. Start with the literal focus adapter. Fit just its existing last-four-layer rank-16 query/value weights for 192 updates of 16 labels. Cycle evenly through descriptions, case tables and compact rules, include both poles, all four later worlds and both displayed orders in each update. Shuffle topic, fitting grammar, name pair and source order deterministically with seed 1481. Train full-vocabulary cross entropy, weighted three on the joint-on world, plus the existing order-specific A/B margin of two. AdamW learning rate 0.0002, no decay, clip one. The frozen encoder never sees a question or options. The recipient gets only slots, the question and the displayed options.

After freezing, score the parent and trained reader on one prewritten new source grammar and two new name pairs, with familiar world and question wording. At each stage test the independently encoded correct state, its opposed counterpart on exactly the same question, zero slots, and the literal teacher text as a separate reference. Require at least 90% matched native actions, 90% jointly correct original and opposed choices with reversed margins, 12/16 strict units and a 25-point advantage over zero slots at every stage. On failure close this run before a language intervention. If it passes, first check the already frozen two learned maps on this same reader against single-step, erasure and opposed-midpoint controls. Only if those pass the earlier two-map thresholds proceed to the matched language-insertion experiment under a new execution seal. The original independent qualification bank stays sealed.
