# Concrete question operations improve lexical readout while conditional state use remains closed

The frozen lexical teacher answered 125/128 native decisions under concrete task questions, compared with 98/128 for paired generic questions containing the same topic word. The net gain of 27 decisions passed the registered operation-benefit criterion. The full reliability screen remained below threshold because one question family scored 30/32 and only six of eight topics were strict. Separately, action-swap learning with a paraphrase-variance penalty recovered 1,449/2,048 first actions when the topic was supplied, essentially the same as the two comparison heads. A genuine conditional source/case/rule audit scored near its zero-slot control through raw E. Its three-stage gate failed, so the conditional route did not advance to learned G. ## Source action identity with the role head frozen

All 4,608 fitting states came from the exposed six expressions per pole and the existing two-field record schema. Each new fitting pair kept the verdict sequence fixed and exchanged the actions. The baseline action head penalized paired midpoints and emphasized action-swap differences. The invariant candidate added the registered within-stratum paraphrase penalty. The Section 6.16 verdict head and topic router were frozen. Both new artifacts were saved, reloaded and replayed on every fitting state before any fresh action source was encoded.

The fresh panel crossed four new expressions per pole in all eight topics, both verdict vocabularies, both field placements, both policies and both verdict orders. Predictions received one E tensor. Supplying the topic was an explicit diagnostic condition with its own admission decision.

## Teacher response to the requested operation

The question comparison kept the topic word, canonical lexical teacher, row renderer, option phrases and A/B assignments fixed. Four newly authored families contrasted a generic preference request with a concrete task operation. Both poles and both displayed option orders were retained for all eight topics. The concrete predicates changed words and lengths, so this measurement concerns those complete question phrases.

The concrete questions recovered 28 native decisions that generic questions missed and lost one generic success. Their paired advantage was 21.09 percentage points. All four families improved, by five, eight, seven and seven decisions. Five of eight topics improved and the other three tied. The registered ten-point benefit criterion passed. Leaving out any one topic gave advantages from 16.96 to 24.11 points, a descriptive check on this bank.

Concrete native family counts were 31/32, 32/32, 32/32 and 30/32. All 128 outputs were A/B, with one forced-logit tie. The concrete common-mean control also produced 128 A/B labels but had four forced ties. The 95% reliability rule required at least 31/32 in every family and seven strict topics. The final family and the 6/8 strict count missed those requirements. The positive question-operation effect and the failed reliability verdict are retained separately. ## Independent conditional source, case and rule states

The final audit used a separate conditional policy fixture with two neutral Boolean flags. One policy chose the plus action exactly when both flags were true and the minus action otherwise. Its opposed policy reversed the assignment. The source described the condition, the case teacher enumerated all four worlds and the rule teacher reintegrated the condition. Every teacher was encoded independently before the downstream world question. Source and case teachers each had two real order variants, while each canonical rule had one realization. This produced 32 source, 32 case and 16 rule states.

The frozen native model received each world and both A/B assignments. Routes were literal text, raw question-free E and zero slots. No world-specific lexical teacher or decoded topic/pole replaced the conditional state.

| Independently authored stage | Literal native correct | Raw E native correct | Zero-slot native correct | Raw E strict topics |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source | 219/256 | 127/256 | 128/256 | 0/8 |
| Exhaustive cases | 215/256 | 128/256 | 128/256 | 0/8 |
| Reintegrated rule | 88/128 | 64/128 | 64/128 | 0/8 |

All three E routes failed the required 90% decisions, 75% strict topics and ten-point lead over zero slots. The literal route also had limited reliability on these conditional questions. The fixed action vocabulary and the improved unconditional lexical questions did not admit a native reader for these conditional states. The source, case and rule computations supply distinct tests because their answer changes with the supplied world.
