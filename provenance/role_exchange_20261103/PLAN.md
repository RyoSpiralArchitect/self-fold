## Fitting the contrast

Flatten each 8 by 2,304 state. Subtract its fitting-bank feature mean and divide by its fitting-bank standard deviation, with the previous one-percent floor. The prior topic router uses its own frozen normalization. Code each first action as a = +1 for the plus action and −1 for the minus action. Order the members of each matched pair by whether the first record accepts its action. Let u be the mean of their standardized states and d half the accepting-first minus rejecting-first difference. For an affine readout f(x) = xw+b fit squared error at u to the mean of the two targets, plus four times the squared error of dw against their half-difference. Center the midpoint features and targets to fit the intercept. Ridge is 0.01 times half the mean squared Euclidean norm of a centered singleton state in that solve, floor 1e−8. Use a float64 dual solve, then save float32 coefficients. No model or optimizer updates and no hyperparameter sweep.

The shared first-role head uses midpoint target zero and contrast target +1. Within each fitting topic the first-action head uses midpoint target a and contrast target zero. A direct policy comparator uses midpoint target zero and contrast target a. The primary prediction uses the sign of the shared role score times the first-action score for the topic inferred by the frozen router. Exact ties abstain. A matched ordinary control uses the identical examples, targets, router, ridge, model class and fit budget with contrast weight one. With this scaling, that arm is algebraically identical to ridge on individual records. A seeded null flips the role orientation of exactly half the training pairs in every topic/vocabulary/placement/first-action stratum, preserving the first-action target. Also score the Section 6.14 reader and the inferred first-action-only shortcut.

## Decisions and closure

The primary source-recovery claim requires at least 95% fitting complete-policy accuracy and 90% strict fitting units. In each fresh vocabulary/placement cell require 90% complete-policy accuracy, 75% strict units spanning both poles and clause orders, and 90% correctly directed two-member exchanges both at fixed action order and at fixed first-role order. Also require at least ten percentage points over both the shuffled-role reader and first-mention shortcut for complete policy in every fresh cell. Neither literal correctness nor an oracle topic can remove a case. Report topic, first-action and first-role signs, both components correct, two-error cancellations, paired rank, correctly and inversely switched pairs, same predictions and abstentions separately. Diagnose whether paired ranking survives when both individual decisions fail the decision boundary. Ranking uses both scores only after independent predictions have been saved.

A predeclared narrower result is recovery of the first record's role alone on unfamiliar action wording. In every fresh cell require at least 95% singleton role accuracy, both role signs correct on at least 90% of the fixed-action-order pairs, and twenty points above the shuffled-role score. This result alone says which verdict was written first. The extra benefit of weighting pairs requires at least ten percentage points over the ordinary fit in both complete-policy accuracy and correctly switched fixed-action-order pairs on the entire fresh panel. Report gains and losses on the same keys. These three verdicts are separate.

Measure full-vocabulary literal greedy accuracy, forced A/B, ties, and both-label correctness on the entire held-out panel using the first Section 6.15 bare question, both displayed label orders and each literal source. These are native decisions under a question, while the primary probe predicts a policy internally before seeing any question. The run does not evaluate a source-to-native composition. A full source pass can advance to a separately sealed reader/recipient study. If only the first-role criterion passes, retain that scoped result and localize the remaining error to action identity, topic or their combination using the independent counts. If neither passes, close this readout and examine whether any paired direction transfers before changing E. An ordinary-fit pass without the weighting effect supports a fitted within-schema readout and gives no advantage claim for the weighting.

One predetermined fit per weight and one null at seed 47. The model allowance is 12,000 forwards, four million processed tokens and 1,200 wall seconds from host initialization. Preserve all failures, the source seal, parameters, pairing assignment, raw scores and denominators. No restart or alternative model selection on fresh outcomes.
