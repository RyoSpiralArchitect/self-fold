# Reference audit

Checked 2026-11-01. The bibliography covers the references already discussed, including negative and contested findings. Metadata verification is not full methodological endorsement. ArXiv links are unversioned unless stated; pin consulted versions before submission. The review status below is a dated observation, not a live feed.

## Important correction

Dadfar (2026) must be read alongside McCardle's open replication submission. The latter reports confounding and reproduction failures; the audit did not establish an accepted final adjudication. SELF-FOLD therefore treats vocabulary/activation correspondence as contested, never as its premise. Length, phase and output-mode controls belong in the protocol.

### `dadfar2026examine`

arXiv preprint; replication critique also recorded.

Open ReScience review submission, NOT asserted accepted.
